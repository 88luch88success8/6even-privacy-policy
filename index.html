Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 29, 2026 • Last updated: November 2026
Key Summary: Your privacy is our highest priority. Screen frames and camera feeds are processed entirely in volatile device memory (RAM) using on-device optical character recognition. No screenshots, screen recordings, or camera images are ever saved to storage or transmitted to external servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how 6even Screen Translator handles information when you use screen, comic, game, and camera translation services on Android devices. The app operates under strict privacy-preserving principles, ensuring user content remains secure and locally confined.

2. Data Processing & Storage

  • Screen Translation (MediaProjection): When screen translation is activated, the app captures on-screen visual content solely to perform real-time optical character recognition (OCR) using on-device engines (Google ML Kit and Tesseract OCR). Captured image frames exist only in volatile RAM during text recognition and are immediately released. No screen captures or video recordings are written to device storage or sent to external servers.
  • Camera Translation: Live camera preview is analyzed strictly on-device in real time to locate and extract text. Camera frames are not photographed, stored on the file system, or transmitted over the internet.
  • Text Translation Data: When on-device translation is selected, text processing remains entirely offline on your device. When optional cloud translation services are enabled, only the recognized text strings are transmitted over secure HTTPS (TLS 1.3) connections to complete translation. No personal identifiers or images are attached.
  • On-Demand OCR & Translation Models: Core scripts (Latin, Cyrillic, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Devanagari) are recognized out of the box. When the user selects an additional script or offline language pack, the app downloads the corresponding static recognition or translation model (e.g., official Tesseract .traineddata or ML Kit packages) over HTTPS into private app storage. No personal data is transmitted during model downloads.
  • Local Translation History: Saved translations and favorites are stored strictly locally in a private database on your device. This data is never synchronized to cloud servers and can be deleted by you at any time with a single tap.
  • User API Keys (BYOK): Any personal API keys provided by the user are stored locally in the hardware-backed Android KeyStore with AES-256-GCM encryption and are used exclusively for direct requests to the respective provider.

3. Android Permissions

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION: Required by Android to run the user-initiated screen capture session and display an ongoing notification with quick Pause and Stop controls.
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW ("Display over other apps"): Required to display floating translation cards and controls over games, comics, and applications.
  • CAMERA: Required only when using the Camera Translation feature to translate text from physical documents or signs.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display the persistent foreground service notification while translation is active.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network connectivity, download on-demand offline OCR and translation language models, query cloud translation services (when selected), and display advertising. The app does not request or use local network (LAN), location, contacts, or storage permissions.

4. Advertising & Analytics

To provide core translation services free of charge, the application integrates certified third-party advertising networks and analytics partners (such as Google AdMob and other certified ad platforms). These partners may collect and process non-personal diagnostic information, device specifications, approximate country-level location, and the Google Advertising ID (GAID) in accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and applicable privacy regulations.

You can manage, opt out of personalized ads, or reset your Advertising ID at any time in Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. User Rights & Data Deletion

Because we do not maintain remote user accounts, your translation data remains entirely on your device. You can permanently erase all local translation history at any time using the Clear History button inside the app, or by clearing application storage in system settings.
